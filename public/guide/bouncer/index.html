Run the door with Yuno Pro

You got an email inviting you to scan tickets at a night. Here is everything you need, in three parts.

Before the night · once 1. Set up your access 5 minutes on your iPhone. No password. At the door 2. Scan each guest Point the camera at their QR code. At the door 3. Follow the colour Green: in. Yellow or red: stop. Orange: you decide.
Before the night · once

1. Set up your access

You need your iPhone and access to the email address the invitation was sent to. Do it before the doors open.

Swipe to see the next step →
The link opened in Safari? Install Yuno Pro, then tap 2. Open this invitation in Yuno Pro on that page. No iPhone? Tap Continue in the browser: you can scan from any phone.
At the door

2. Scan each guest

On the night, open Yuno Pro and tap the card with the name of the night. One ticket is one person: each guest shows their own QR code.

At the door

3. Follow the colour

Each colour has one meaning. Read the big words at the top of the card.

Green + yellow bannerLet in + drink

Let the person in and give the free drink (or the token the club uses).

GreenLet in

Let the person in. No drink with this ticket.

YellowStop

Already used at the time shown. Do not let in. Call the manager if they insist.

OrangeYou decide

Late guest. Follow the club's rule: see Late guest.

RedStop

Not valid (the reason is on the card). Try their name in the list first.

Orange card

Late guest

Some tickets are only valid before a time, for example “Before 1 AM”. The card shows the limit and how late the guest is, and waits for your choice.

If something goes wrong

Problems and fixes

The code email does not arrive

Wait one minute, check your spam folder, then tap “Send a new code”. You can ask for 4 codes every 15 minutes. Always use the most recent code.

“This invitation was sent to …”

You are signed in with another email address. Tap “Use another account”, then sign in with the address the invitation was sent to.

The camera does not start

Open the iPhone Settings, then Yuno Pro, and turn on Camera. Come back and tap “Start Scanning”.

The network drops

Keep scanning. The phone checks tickets against its own copy of the list and sends the scans when the network is back. The status shows “Offline” meanwhile.

A guest with a red card insists they paid

Search their name in the List tab. If they are not there, ask for their confirmation email and call the club manager. Never let someone in on a red card without the manager's OK.

“This night is over: the invitation no longer works”

An invitation is for one night and stops 6 hours after it ends. Ask the club for a new one for the next night.
